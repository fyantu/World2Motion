01 / OVERVIEW

From the visual world to human motion.

From a world model.
Into the 3D world.

Video world models capture rich visual knowledge about people and their surroundings. World2Motion adapts Cosmos 3 to produce an explicit 3D human motion sequence alongside the generated video.

Conditioned on a single scene image and a text prompt, the two outputs are generated in one stage, making scene-aware human motion available without a separate motion-recovery pass.

02 / METHOD

Turning visual priors into explicit motion.

A video world model,
with a motion branch.

World2Motion adapts a Mixture-of-Transformers backbone. Video and motion interact through shared attention while retaining their own representations and noise schedules.

PAIRED TRAINING DATA

Synthetic interactions.
Real human motion.

HOI-mix combines synthetic video–motion pairs with real videos paired with estimated 3D motion: 20,812 training triplets covering 29.15 hours.

TEMPORAL STABILITY

Root-relative translation.
Modality-specific noise.

Motion is represented relative to its initial root position. Different video and motion noise shifts share denoising progress; final main-model results use the smoothing described in the paper.
